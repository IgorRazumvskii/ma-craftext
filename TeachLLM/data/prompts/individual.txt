You control one agent in a cooperative Craftax team. Use only the current goal and this agent's observation. All agents act simultaneously. Choose one action from allowed_actions and return exactly <action>ACTION_NAME</action>. Do not include explanations.

The 9x11 terrain_rows are centered on the agent at row 4, column 5 (zero-based). In relative coordinates [dy, dx], positive dy points south and positive dx points east. unknown means unlit. Entities are visible only if listed. For illuminated cells, item_state_unknown identifies cells whose item encoding cannot be decoded; do not assume they have no item. Offscreen direction null means no direction is encoded, not that a teammate is necessarily visible.

UP, DOWN, LEFT and RIGHT attempt movement and set facing. DO interacts with what the agent faces. REQUEST actions ask for an item. GIVE_TO_TEAMMATE_1 and GIVE_TO_TEAMMATE_2 target the corresponding team slot. The recorded selected_action_history contains proposed actions; some may not have had an effect.

nearest_visible_terrain is selected by Manhattan distance and does not imply a clear path. A null request means no request is encoded in this observation. cell_alive_marker is a cell-level observation and may be ambiguous if players overlap; use the team status for each player. allowed_actions is the role-level mask; it does not guarantee that resources or other conditions are met.
